Sudoku shouldn't come with a sales pitch.

It's nine rows, nine columns and a pencil. Somehow most sudoku apps turned that into video ads, weekly subscriptions and "out of lives" screens. We built the other kind.

So what's the catch?

Fair question. When something is free, you're usually the product. Here's why that isn't true here.

There's nothing to pay for.

sudo sudoku runs entirely on your iPhone. Puzzles are generated on device, your history lives in one local file, and the app works with no connection at all. We run no servers, so there's no bill for ads to cover.

There's nothing being sold.

The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. There is no analytics SDK, no ad network and no account system, so there is nothing that could collect your data even if we wanted it to. The privacy policy has the details.

You don't have to take our word for it.

The full source code is public under the MIT License. Read it, build it, search it for an ad SDK. You won't find one.

Trust, but grep.

Clone the repository and list everything the app imports:

root@ios:~/sudo-sudoku$ grep -rh '^import' SudoSudoku | sort -u
import Combine
import CoreHaptics
import Foundation
import GameKit
import SwiftUI
import UIKit
root@ios:~/sudo-sudoku$ # six Apple frameworks. that's all of it.

No ad SDKs, no analytics, no payment frameworks, no third-party packages. GameKit powers the optional Game Center leaderboards. Everything else is the game.

Free isn't the feature. The game is.

We didn't strip out the ads to make up for a thin product. Here's what's left when nothing is trying to sell you something:

  • Infinite puzzles, generated on device. Every grid has exactly one solution, so you never have to guess. Nothing comes from a canned database.
  • Four difficulties, from --easy to --master, each scored 0–100 by a logical solver.
  • A real ELO ladder, from SCRIPT_KIDDIE to THE_ARCHITECT, with Game Center leaderboards and eleven achievements.
  • No pressure mechanics. No lives, no fail state. Undo is never punished, and the timer is optional.
  • A full terminal fantasy. Every puzzle generates behind a live breach log, and every win ends in ACCESS GRANTED.
  • Respectful by default. Every animation respects Reduce Motion, and sound is never forced on.
